## FAQ

**Do these fit all controller types?**
Most do. PS5 DualSense grips are thicker than Xbox or Switch Pro, so we noted when reviewers mentioned snug fits. The Kytok and UPERGO have the most reviews confirming they fit all types without issue.

**Will a stand scratch my controller grips?**
The ones with rubber pads and cradles (not clamps) won't. Plastic-on-plastic contact is gentler than plastic gripping your grip.

**How stable are these if I bump the desk?**
The wood options (UPERGO, CUIWHRL) and the weighted metal bases (KDD RGB) are stable. The plastic stacked organizer (Kytok) is light but doesn't tip easily because it's wide at the base.

**Can I use just the headset part if I don't have a controller?**
Most of these work solo, but they're designed as combo stands. If you only need a headset stand, there are cheaper options, but then you're missing the combo value.

**Are these good for streaming?**
If you stream, the UPERGO and CUIWHRL look better on camera. The KDD RGB is good if your whole rig is RGB. The Kytok is functional but doesn't pop visually.
